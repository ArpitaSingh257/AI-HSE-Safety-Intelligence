## 3. Multi-Label Decision Criteria

Annotators must adhere to the following multi-label assignment rules:

1. **Single vs Multi-Label:**
   - Assign **1 Rule** if the initiator and failure mode align directly with a single control (e.g. driver unbuckled during truck rollover $\rightarrow$ *Driving*).
   - Assign **2 Rules** if a physical failure was exacerbated by a secondary position or control bypass (e.g. Crane load dropped while worker stood in exclusion zone $\rightarrow$ *Safe Mechanical Lifting* + *Line of Fire*).
   - Assign **3 Rules** only in complex compound failures (e.g. Hot work inside a vessel on an unisolated fuel gas line $\rightarrow$ *Hot Work* + *Confined Space* + *Energy Isolation*).
2. **The "No Applicable Rule" Class:**
   - If an incident involves general ergonomics, slip on ice, or environmental heat without a high-risk activity covered by the 9 LSRs, assign `verified_primary_lsr = 'None'` and `verified_life_saving_rules = 'None'`.

## 4. Handling Candidate vs Verified Status

```text
[OSHA Narrative]
      │
      ├──> candidate_primary_lsr    (Generated by domain keywords — NOT Ground Truth)
      │
      └──> Annotator Review:
              ├── ACCEPT  ──> verified_primary_lsr = candidate_primary_lsr
              │               lsr_label_type = 'VERIFIED'
              │
              ├── MODIFY  ──> verified_primary_lsr = <Correct IOGP Rule>
              │               lsr_label_type = 'VERIFIED'
              │
              └── REJECT  ──> verified_primary_lsr = 'None'
                              lsr_label_type = 'VERIFIED'
```
